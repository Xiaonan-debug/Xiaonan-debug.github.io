I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at the [University of Notre Dame](https://www.nd.edu/), where I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. [Xiangliang Zhang](https://sites.nd.edu/xiangliang-zhang/). Previously, I was a Student Researcher at [Bake AI](https://bakeai.inc/), and I received my B.S. in Data Science and Computer Science from the [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)](https://hkust.edu.hk/).

My recent first-author work includes **[SecProbe](https://xiaonan-debug.github.io/secprobe-page/)**, an adaptive evaluation framework for coding agents on real-world cybersecurity vulnerabilities; **KITE**, a method for learning from synthetic data without model collapse; and **RefineLab** (AAAI 2026), which automatically optimizes benchmark datasets toward explicit quality targets.

## Research Interests

I study **large language models (LLMs)**, with a focus on:

- **Recursive Self-Improvement**: systems that improve themselves, from self-evolving research agents to models trained on their own data.
- **Cybersecurity**: evaluating and improving coding agents on real-world vulnerabilities.
- **Agents**: long-horizon LLM agents that learn from experience.
- **Post-Training**: reinforcement learning and adaptive reasoning for more capable, reliable models.
- **Synthetic Data**: generating and curating data that targets a model's weaknesses without collapse.
